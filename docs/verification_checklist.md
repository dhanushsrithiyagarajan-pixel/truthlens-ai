# TruthLens AI — Standard 10-Step Verification Protocol
## Investigative Methodology for Digital Media, Online Claims, and Cyber Threat Intelligence
**Tagline:** *"Pause. Investigate. Verify."*  

---

### Purpose and Philosophy
This protocol provides digital investigators, students, and cybersecurity analysts with a repeatable, systematic 10-step methodology to examine online claims before sharing, acting upon, or publishing assessments. **Checklist completion does not automatically equate to proof of authenticity;** rather, it establishes whether evidentiary standards have been satisfied.

#### Step 1: Source & Origin Identification
- **Objective:** Determine the earliest indexed creator, publisher, or distributor of the claim.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Inspect the top-level domain (TLD). Differentiate authentic domains (`.gov.in`, `.ac.in`, `.org`) from high-risk disposable registries (`.top`, `.xyz`, `.click`).
  2. Search exact headline phrases within quotation marks on major news aggregators to locate primary wire reporting.
  3. Scrutinize website "About Us" and editorial governance pages.

#### Step 2: Author Credentials & Timestamp Audit
- **Objective:** Evaluate the expertise of the named author and check the original publication timestamp.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Cross-reference author profiles on Google Scholar, LinkedIn, or ORCID.
  2. Check for missing bylines (anonymous or generic pseudonyms such as "Admin" or "Staff Reporter").
  3. Verify the first published date to prevent outdated articles from being recirculated as breaking news.

#### Step 3: Reverse Media & Keyframe Verification
- **Objective:** Uncover whether embedded imagery has been indexed previously on the web.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Ingest imagery or video frames into Google Lens, TinEye, Yandex Visual Search, or Bing Visual Search.
  2. Utilize TinEye's "Oldest" sort filter to identify the primary origin.
  3. Extract keyframes from video files using tools like InVID-WeVerify.

#### Step 4: Original Context & Chronology Check
- **Objective:** Verify whether genuine historical media is being falsely re-captioned for a current geopolitical event.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Compare the geographic background, street signs, and weather conditions with the claimed event location.
  2. Confirm whether vehicles, military uniforms, or infrastructure match the purported national setting.

#### Step 5: Independent Multi-Source Corroboration
- **Objective:** Verify if the factual assertion is corroborated by at least two independent, accredited news wires.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Check coverage across reputable national and international wires (e.g., Reuters, Associated Press, PTI, ANI).
  2. Cross-reference specialized fact-checking repositories (PIB Fact Check, Alt News, BOOM Live, Snopes).

#### Step 6: Primary Document & Citation Audit
- **Objective:** Verify official legal, regulatory, or scientific claims against primary government gazettes or clinical repositories.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Search official government portals (`egazette.gov.in`, `rbi.org.in`, `who.int`).
  2. For medical breakthrough claims, audit clinical trial registries (CTRI, ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed).
  3. Verify whether cited "internal memos" or "leaked orders" possess verifiable circular numbers.

#### Step 7: Visual Artifacts & Deepfake Inspection
- **Objective:** Inspect visual media for diffusion model synthesis artifacts and compression anomalies.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Run Error Level Analysis (ELA) using TruthLens Deepfake Lab to inspect compression quantization.
  2. Check anatomical coherence: eye specular highlights, ear symmetry, teeth regularity, and hand anatomy.
  3. Look for background architectural warping and lighting vector discrepancies.

#### Step 8: Emotional Manipulation & Panic Audit
- **Objective:** Analyze psychological triggers and persuasive linguistic patterns.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Identify manufactured urgency cues ("Forward immediately", "Act before midnight", "Secret leak").
  2. Evaluate hyperbolic adjectives and outrage-inducing framing designed to bypass critical deliberation.

#### Step 9: Cyber Scam & Phishing Indicators
- **Objective:** Assess whether the claim functions as a lure for financial fraud, credential harvesting, or malware deployment.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. Check for unsolicited requests for OTPs, Aadhaar linking, or payment gateway transfers.
  2. Look for unauthorized `.apk` installation prompts distributed via instant messaging platforms.
  3. Audit domain security and typosquatting using the TruthLens Source Credibility Explorer.

#### Step 10: Final Verdict, Dossier Compilation & Reporting
- **Objective:** Synthesize findings into one of 5 standard academic verdicts and formulate responsible action recommendations.
- **Verdicts:**
  - *Supported by available evidence*
  - *Contradicted by available evidence*
  - *Misleading or missing context*
  - *Unverified — more evidence required*
  - *Suspicious indicators detected*
- **Action:** If the claim constitutes fraud or cybercrime, report it to the National Cyber Crime Portal (`cybercrime.gov.in` / Helpline `1930`).
