> 
> Modern generative AI can swap faces and synthesize realistic voiceovers in seconds. But synthetic media still leaves clues if you know where to look:  
>

### Post 2: The "Urgent Electricity Disconnection" Phishing Trap
**Platform Focus:** WhatsApp Community Channels / Twitter (X) / Facebook  
**Visual Framing:** Screenshot of a realistic fake SMS ("Your electricity will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill...") with bold red annotation arrows highlighting red flags: personal mobile contact, high panic deadline, unofficial `.apk` link.  

**Post Copy:**  
> 🚨 FRAUD ALERT: "Your electricity will be cut off tonight at 9:30 PM!" 🚨  
> 
> Have you received an SMS claiming your power bill is unpaid and urging you to call an "officer" or install an app immediately?  
> 
> ⚠️ **THIS IS A PHISHING SCAM.**  
> 
> State electricity discoms NEVER:  
> ❌ Ask you to dial personal 10-digit mobile numbers.  
> ❌ Demand payment via private UPI handles or Telegram links.  
> ❌ Distribute unverified `.apk` files via SMS or WhatsApp.  
> 
> ✅ **WHAT TO DO:**  
> 1. Do NOT click the link or install any APK.  
> 2. Log in directly to your official electricity board portal or utility app to check your actual account status.  
> 3. If money was fraudulently debited, immediately dial **1930** (National Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline) or report to **cybercrime.gov.in** within the golden hour to freeze funds!  
> 
> Protect your family—forward this to your apartment and neighborhood WhatsApp groups today!  
>

### Post 3: The Myth of the HTTPS "Padlock"
**Platform Focus:** Twitter (X) / LinkedIn  
**Visual Framing:** Sleek graphic illustrating a web browser address bar with an HTTPS lock icon next to a deceptive domain `rbi-update-notice.top`. Text callout: *"Encryption ≠ Truth"*.  

**Post Copy:**  
> Think that little padlock in your browser means the news is true? Think again. 🔒❌  
> 
> A common cybersecurity misconception is that **HTTPS = Trustworthy Information**.  
> 
> Here’s the reality:  
> 🌐 **HTTPS ONLY means your connection is encrypted.** It prevents local eavesdroppers from seeing what you type.  
> ⚠️ Today, over **80% of phishing and fake news websites use free HTTPS certificates.**  
> 
> A scammer can register a lookalike domain like `pib-subsidy-funds.xyz`, install a free SSL certificate in 2 minutes, and look completely "secure" to an untrained eye.  
> 
> **How to protect yourself:**  
> • Inspect the domain suffix: Beware of disposable TLDs (`.top`, `.xyz`, `.click`, `.buzz`).  
> • Look for official government TLDs: In India, authentic government sites end in `.gov.in` or `.nic.in`.  
> • Always ask: *Who is publishing this, and what is their editorial provenance?*  
> 
> Don't let a green padlock fool you.  
> *Pause. Investigate. Verify.*  
> 
> #CyberLiteracy #WebSecurity #InfosecTips #PhishingAwareness #TruthLensAI

### Post 4: How to Run a Reverse Image Search in 30 Seconds
**Platform Focus:** Instagram Infographic / Twitter (X) Thread  
**Visual Framing:** Clean 3-step visual infographic showing an image being dragged into Google Lens, revealing the identical photo published 4 years prior during a different event.  

**Post Copy:**  
> Did that dramatic photo really happen this morning? Or was it recycled from 2019? 📸🔄  
> 
> Over 60% of viral visual misinformation consists of authentic, historical photographs republished with false breaking headlines.  
> 
> **Here is your 30-second verification superpower: Reverse Image Search!**  
> 
> 📱 **On Mobile:**  
> 1. Long-press the image in Google Chrome and tap *"Search image with Google"* (or open Google Lens app).  
> 2. Inspect the search results: Look at the earliest publication dates.  
> 
> 💻 **On Desktop:**  
> 1. Open [images.google.com] or [tineye.com].  
> 2. Click the camera icon and paste the image URL or upload the photo.  
> 3. In TinEye, sort by *"Oldest"* to pinpoint the true origin and original photographer.  
> 
> Before you hit "Retweet" or forward to your family group, spend 30 seconds running a reverse search. Stop rumors at your doorstep!  
>

### Post 5: The "Task-Based Part-Time Job" Telegram Scam
**Platform Focus:** LinkedIn / Instagram Reel / Facebook  
**Visual Framing:** Split graphic: Promising WhatsApp screenshot ("Earn ₹5,000/day by liking YouTube videos!") paired with a warning stamp: *"Prepayment Fraud Architecture"*.  

**Post Copy:**  
> "Earn ₹5,000 daily from home! Just like YouTube videos and rate hotels on Google Maps!" Sounds tempting, right? 💸📉  
> 
> Be warned: This is the **Task-Based Prepayment Scam**, currently draining millions from students and job seekers across India.  
> 
> **How the scam unfolds:**  
> 1️⃣ **The Bait:** You complete 3 simple tasks and they actually transfer ₹150–₹300 to your UPI to build trust.  
> 2️⃣ **The Trap:** They invite you to a "VIP Telegram Channel" where lucrative tasks require a ₹2,000 "crypto deposit" or "security fee".  
> 3️⃣ **The Drain:** You see a fake digital dashboard showing massive "profits", but when you try to withdraw, they demand additional "tax clearance" fees of ₹50,000+.  
> 4️⃣ **The Ghosting:** Once you refuse to pay more, they block you instantly.  
> 
> 🛑 **Golden Rule:** Legitimate companies NEVER ask job applicants to pay money to unlock employment tasks.  
> 
> If you or a friend have been targeted, report immediately to **cybercrime.gov.in** or call **1930**.
