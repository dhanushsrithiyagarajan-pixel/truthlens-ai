Slide 02 / The Threat Landscape

The Synthetic Media Crisis & Cognitive Exploitation

Generative AI models have democratized the creation of hyper-realistic deceptive media, rendering traditional visual intuition obsolete.

1. Asymmetric Synthetic Threats

• Acoustic Voice Cloning: Fueling "virtual kidnapping" and CEO imposter wire transfers.

• Neural Lip-Syncing: Fabricating political confessions without leaving classic blur seams.

• Recycled Visual Media: Re-circulating authentic 2019 footage under false 2026 breaking news captions.

2. The Forensic Vulnerability

• Social media platforms automatically strip EXIF camera metadata upon upload.

• Automated AI detectors are prone to catastrophic generalization errors and false alarms.

• Critical Gap: Digital citizens lack a structured, multi-source verification protocol.

Slide 06 / Governance & Ethics

Ethics, Limitations & Human-in-the-Loop

The Limitations of Automated AI

• False Positives: Legitimate compressed media frequently triggers false alarms.

• Adversarial Evasion: Micro-noise injection bypasses neural discriminators.

• Doctrine: Algorithmic scores are advisory, never definitive judicial proof.

Responsible Implementation

• Zero Deceptive Generation: TruthLens does not generate deepfakes of real people.

• Indian Regulatory Integration: Direct links & protocols for National Cyber Helpline 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in.
